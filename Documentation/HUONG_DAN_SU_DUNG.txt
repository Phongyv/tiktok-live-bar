ONG CHU MMO LIVE - HUONG DAN SU DUNG

1. Nhan dup run.bat.
2. Cho game mo va hien NODE ONLINE.
3. Thu nhanh bang DEMO 30 hoac DEMO 200.
4. Ket noi live that: nhap TikTok username dang live, khong nhap dau @, roi bam KET NOI.

KET NOI LIVE THAT

Nguoi xem gui chat, like, follow, share hoac gift se thay NPC tren san.
Ten va avatar TikTok duoc cap nhat tu dong; neu TikTok chua tra anh, game dung avatar du phong de khong bi o den.
Neu Node hoac duong truyen bi mat tam thoi, game va cau noi TikTok se tu ket noi lai.
Khong can dong game khi bi rot mang ngan han.

PHIM TAT

F1  An/hien bang dieu khien.
F2  Bat/tat nen xanh chroma de ghep TikTok LIVE Studio.
F11 Bat/tat toan man hinh.

LENH CHAT

nhay / dance   Nhan vat nhay.
di vong / walk Nhan vat nhay buoc tai cho, khong roi vi tri.
doi nv         Doi nhan vat.

KHI CO GIFT

Camera zoom vao dung vi tri cua nguoi tang qua.
Top 1, Top 2 va Top 3 dung tren ba buc rieng ngay truoc ban DJ.
Nguoi choi binh thuong luon mo va trong suot de tao cam giac dam dong.
Khi mot nguoi tang qua, nguoi do se sang ro va camera zoom lai.
Nguoi tang hien ten, avatar, banner, canh va hieu ung theo gia tri gift.
Sau khi hieu ung ket thuc, nguoi khong thuoc Top 3 se tro lai trang thai mo.

TIKTOK LIVE STUDIO

Capture cua so OngChuMMO_Live.exe bang Game Capture hoac Window Capture.
Neu muon ghep len video, bam F2 va dung Chroma Key mau xanh trong TikTok LIVE Studio.

DJ MUSIC PLAYER

1. Tha file MP3, WAV, OGG hoac M4A vao thu muc DJ_MUSIC.
2. Mo Control Panel, chon tab DJ Music va bam Quet thu vien.
3. Copy Music Source URL, trong TikTok LIVE Studio chon Add source > Link va dan URL.
4. Bam Phat nhac trong Control Panel. Neu trang Link hien nut Bat am thanh LIVE, bam nut do mot lan ngay trong nguon Link.
5. Sau do co the chon bai, tam dung, chuyen bai, bat ngau nhien/lap va chinh am luong tu Control Panel.
Nhac phat trong nguon Link cua LIVE Studio; Unity Game khong con tu phat nhac.

VIDEO DJ

Tha file MP4, MOV, M4V hoac WebM vao thu muc DJ_VIDEO.
Game tu phat lap video tren man hinh sau ban DJ va luon tat am thanh video.
Nen dung video 16:9, do phan giai 1280x720 hoac 1920x1080.
